## Motion personality
Calm, product-UI motion on an empty light canvas: every layout is built from the background with short ease-out entrances (6-14 frames), held (the opening icons keep a slow sine float, the send button gives a 10-frame click) while text appears in one-frame steps (characters at 1.5-1.8 f, words at 2-7 f), and removed with a 9-10 frame ease-in fade that starts on a beat, so scenes change every 8 beats. Energy comes from counting and multiplying (an accelerating counter, a stepped pull-back over a growing hexagon network, packets hopping between nodes and cards) rather than from big camera moves; the one large move is the final zoom-through that turns the network's centre cell into the logo, followed by a character-like blink and a lockup slide.
