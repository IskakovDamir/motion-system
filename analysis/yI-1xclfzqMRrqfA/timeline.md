## Motion personality
Precise and quiet: entrances are one-frame pops into pre-laid, centred layouts (words, lines, single-word cards) or small grow-fades about an element's own centre, exits are short ease-in fades in place to an empty background, and no element slides in on its own (only the pull-back carries lattice nodes in across the frame edges). Continuous life comes from dots that hop between nodes or windows, each hop triggering the next change, and from one strictly linear logo marquee, while everything that travels (the log-scale network pull-back, the packet hops, the hexagon gather and collapse, the lockup slide) shares one symmetric ease-in-out with equal durations, so farther elements simply move faster. Layout changes keep to the beat grid (exits start 5-11 f after a beat, the next layout lands 6-10 f before the following one), element staggers are tight (1.8-3 f, centre-out or left to right) while words pop 3-13 f apart, and the finale turns the logo row itself into the brand mark through shared hexagons.
