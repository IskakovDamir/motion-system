## Motion personality (v8c4 view)
Every change of layout is carried by one shared element: a 3D screen stack becomes a flat page, an avatar becomes a pair of mascots, a mascot swarm collapses into one mascot that lands in the next avatar slot, a phone card zooms to the centre. Moves start with long ease-ins and end with fast ease-outs or springy pops (overshoot 3-20%; the split pair comes out 50% oversized and shrinks back), text streams letter by letter with soft fronts and a passing shimmer, and the picture is almost never cut (one hidden cut inside a push-up, on the beat).
