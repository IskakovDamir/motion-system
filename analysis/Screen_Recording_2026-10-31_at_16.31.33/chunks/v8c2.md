## Motion personality (v8c2 view)
A product UI shown as a sequence of zoomed views that never stop moving: lists and chat feeds scroll in one-row steps on the off-beat, each new row announced by a mascot that darts in from the lower left and a label or message that streams in left to right with a soft trailing gradient. Every view enters already moving and settles with the same strong ease-out (about bezier(0.16, 1.05, 0.91, 0.95), 20-24 f) and leaves with a strong ease-in into a cut on motion (whip, punch-in or slide); the middle of the chunk swaps the flat UI for a 3D card yaw and a rapid card-deck flip (a card every 4 f) with an accelerating push-in and an ease-out pull-back, framed by a status pill that drops in from the upper right and gets a page-white shimmer sweep (ease-in-out, 15-18 f) each time it comes to rest. Small life runs underneath: a faint dot grid scrolls behind the deck at one constant rate whatever the camera does, and the mascots' glyph eyes glance sideways or up and down inside their still bodies.
