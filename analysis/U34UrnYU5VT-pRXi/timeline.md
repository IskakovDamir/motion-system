## Motion personality
Flat light-grey cards cut hard on the music (5-10 frames after a beat from the third cut on), and each layout is built in place and then held: text arrives word by word in single-frame pops into a pre-laid line, UI items, icons and mascots pop or rise-fade on fast even staggers, and every UI screen creeps forward under a slow ease-out push-in that starts at full speed in its first frame and is stretched to the segment's length. The hex mascot carries the life: honeycombs and crowds of mascots pop in (on a radial front or at random), their eyes glance and blink in single moves or waves, and the big mascot pops in, slides into its lockup and closes the film with a tilted scale-in. The few large moves are kept for the transitions (a globe collapse into a point, logos converging into a mascot row, mascots flying into UI avatar slots, a vertical scroll-push), and all but the asymmetric collapse share one symmetric ease-in-out around bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1) over 16-23 frames, the same curve as the lockup slide and the page scroll.
