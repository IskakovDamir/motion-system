## Motion personality
Product-UI storytelling on a flat light-grey canvas: each scene is built in under half a second with ease-out fades that carry a small settle (words rise 18 px, windows rise 30 px and grow 3%, headlines drop 10-12 px, a hub settles from 111%), then the UI plays out in one-frame steps (typed letters, terminal lines, label swaps, list jumps) punctuated by short 11-14 frame rise-fades and a 9-frame button press. Exits are short accelerating fades with a 3-6% scale change, about every 8-10 beats, and two of the three scene changes land a key frame on a strong onset. The connective tissue is small travelling dots: ambient hops between agent icons at the start, a fast 6-frame dispatch cascade and a slower 16-17-frame return cadence in the hub, all on the same 22-24 frame ease-in-out.
