## Motion personality
Product-UI storytelling on one flat light-grey canvas that keeps pulling back: each scene is built in under half a second from ease-out fades that carry a small settle (words and letters rise 17-18 px, windows rise 30 px and grow 3%, headlines drop 10-12 px, the hub settles from 111%), the UI then plays out in one-frame steps (typed letters, terminal lines, label swaps, list jumps, spinner glyphs) punctuated by 11-14 frame rise-fades and a 9-frame button press, and layouts leave with short accelerating fades, some with a 3-6% scale change. The connective tissue is small dots that pop on, travel one symmetric ease-in-out of 22-32 frames whatever the distance and pop off, from the first hops between agent icons to the dense traffic of the 12-team grid, while the camera pulls back twice on slow 53-54 frame S-curves and new teams ripple in 3 frames apart. The finale swaps UI for pure geometry: a radial wave turns every card into a hexagon, the hexagons swarm into a honeycomb on the music's strong onsets and fold ring by ring into one cell, and a jump cut wakes that cell up as the brand mark for a calm lockup whose letters rise 2 frames apart before a 2-second static hold.
