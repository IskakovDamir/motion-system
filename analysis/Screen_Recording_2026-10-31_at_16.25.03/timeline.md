## Motion personality
A light, dotted near-white stage on which every layout is assembled piece by piece and never quite stops: letters fly in from the frame edges or type on one at a time (0.6-5 f per letter), sometimes each with its own small pop (a glyph that splits in two, one that spins in from a speck, dots that jump out of their stems and drop back with a bounce), words whip or rise in 5-15 f apart, entrances arrive oversized and shrink to rest or land past rest, and holds keep a slow 0.03-0.06% per frame zoom drift while the background blobs breathe or fade in behind them (in the 3D stack a code view keeps scrolling to its first line and single layers slide down on their own between the camera moves). The moves come in a few sharp shapes: steep in-outs with long still ends and most of the travel in a few blurred pictures (word rises, line splits, the phone punch-in, the dial), pure ease-ins that leave through a frame edge or a pull-back and are cut at their fastest picture, and fast ease-outs for entrances; speed itself is animated, with the card scroll, the grid pull-back and the counter ramping from a crawl to a burst and back, and a stutter of stepped punch-ins as one exit. Transitions carry the motion instead of cutting it: vertical pushes and horizontal whips keep one direction across a hidden swap, a line splits open to make room for the next element, a pulled-back phone zooms through into a grid of phones, and the promo's only two hard cuts land on the fastest picture of an accelerating pull-back.
