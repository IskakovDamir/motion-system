## Motion personality (v6c2)
Nearly every eased move in this half fits a very steep in-out (bezier about (0.8-1.0, 0, 0-0.15, 1)): long still ends and most of the travel in 4-6 frames, used for word rises, line splits and slide-asides, while exits are pure ease-ins cut at their fastest, most blurred picture and entrances are fast ease-outs that land slightly oversized or past rest. Elements arrive one at a time (words 5-6 f apart, lines 7 f apart), holds never stop moving (slow 0.03-0.06% per frame zoom drifts), and speed itself is animated: camera pull-backs and counters ramp from a crawl to a burst and back.
