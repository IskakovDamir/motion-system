## Motion personality
Everything lives on one dark canvas that the camera works hard: tilts, whips and pull-backs (17-33 frames, strongly eased, bezier x1 0.7-0.94) carry the eye between layouts, while text and cards arrive in pairs from opposite sides, either liquid-warped, motion-blurred or with a small overshoot. Fast moves alternate with near-static holds and slow linear drifts, and UI theatre (typed prompt, cursor, click burst, hopping selector) is staged at about one event per beat (32.7 frames).
