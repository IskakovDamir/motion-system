## Motion personality
This stretch sells a product UI like a title sequence: every element arrives as a streak (blurred lines from opposite edges, light streaks with sparkle heads, luminous pixel squares) and settles with stiff, near-critical springs in 13-15 frames, while the camera never rests: slow creeps end in one-picture snap zooms, vertical and horizontal whips (chained without a hold), and a 3D swing with overshoot. Interactions are staged as beats (every clicked chip and chosen row dips to about x0.8 and springs back, a 3D cursor dive and press on the beat, a square-tile shockwave, a flare bloom that the hard cut lands on), even the backgrounds keep moving under held titles, and captions over footage stay soft and constantly drifting.
