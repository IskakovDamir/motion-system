## Motion personality
A dark, quiet canvas where an agent writes: lines type themselves letter by letter in grey that turns white, the logo spins like a thinking indicator, and the camera punches between framings of the same canvas with hidden hard cuts: one-picture scale jumps buried in directional blur, each wrapped in an ease-in pan that either whips through the cut blurred or stops dead on one crisp picture just before it, and a long ease-out glide. Results arrive as UI: a card rises, a grid flashes, a dot ring reveals footage, a cursor drags it out and drops it into a folder on the beat, and every layout leaves with a whip (left or up) into a hard cut.
